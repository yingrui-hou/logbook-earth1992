Since the first time I saw tartelette aux citrons (TAC), this lovely yellow and white dessert, in the British movie *"Toast"*, I've been interested in this pretty dessert, because I had never tasted any cake that uses lemon as main ingredient. So when I moved to France, this is the first thing on my to-try list, and I was not disappointed, even the most normal tarte de citron, possibly came from the food industry pipeline, still tastes good, perfectly meets my number one need to a dessert: not too sweet, and my number two need: not too milky and creamy. 

After trying some citron desserts here and there, I was inspired and motivated to do somthing more "seriously" with my ok-ish ability of cycling: a taste trip of tartelette aux citrons in Auvergne. And to note a bit this trip and maybe leave some information for another tartelette aux citrons lover, I'll try to gather my "taste report" and give my very-much-biased rate to each of the TAC I try. 

C'est parti!

----

### [Pâtisserie-Chocolaterie Souliere](https://patisserie-chocolaterie-souliere.fr/)
- Address: 2 Bd Gambetta, 63400 Chamalières
- Price (in 2026): 4.6 euro
- Layers: Mernigue italienne, creme citron, gelee citron vert, et pate sablee. 
- Comment: I love it! The meringue balenced perfectly the sourness from citron, making the taste tender and fresh. The pate sablee base is soft inside, with a satisfying texture. The only pity is the TAC itself was a bit petit. 
- Rate: :star: :star: :star: :star: :star:
