Here is the end of 2025!

I’ve done some first-in-my-life things this year 😀

I went on a exciting and crazy trip this summer. At the beginning, I was hesitating which one I should choose, a cozy week in Normandie or a “cool” week in Svalbard (Spitsbergen). At some point, an idea just come in my mind, “why not doing the both?”, yes, pourquoi pas?

So the plan of the first part was to do a cycling trip from Paris to Le Havre, alongside La Seine. This was the first road trip I did, a bikepacking trip without camping XD (yeah, I don’t know how to camp yet, need to improve this to save some money on the road trip!). Honestly, the Paris part is a bit awful, some part of road is single path, surrounded by the bushes and trees, I was really afraid that some one coming from the opposite direction and we met in the middle of this ~2km-long single path. Luckily, this didn’t happen! Besides, some part of the route passed some “dangerous” area, some people just gathered next to the route, drinking and smoking weeds (yes, I smelt it, really don’t like it 😦 ), and this made me feel unsafe, so I had to accelerate a bit and leave quickly.

But things got much better after Mante La Jolie, or say, outside Paris region and into the Normandie region. The route condition is much better, the view as well, beautiful and quiet villages along the river. I’ve seen a lot of different kinds of beautiful birds when I was somewhere in the wild, next to the river and lakes, on my bike. Those are the moments I wanted for a bike trip, and I really got the luck to have good weather to enjoy these, thank god!

Besides the views, and the bitterness and sweetness of cycling, I’ve met some really nice hosts on the road. The first host was a kind old couple, in the small city next to the Paris centre. They’ve hosted many bike travellers and they really like to do it. After arriving around 20h00 (thank for the long day during summer), they kindly offer some food for as my dinner for free, we had some relaxing chats, by my poor french, english and translator :D. The place was really nice, a full functional bathroom, some free drinks (coke is really helpful after a long riding!) and comfortable bed of course with AC! Then the morning of the day after, the breakfast is unexpectedly good, I got full set of sweet things and salty things, croissant, pain aux chocolats, fruits (mirabelles! ), jams, hams, bagguette, I felt that I ate like a king. I’ve even got a box of mirabelles (from their own garden) when I told them mirabelle is my favourite summer fruit (it is!). Another one is a single lady, her place is in a small village, on the top of a hill, I really spent all my energy to climb that steep slop to reach her lovely place (and I’ll never regret!). She has a lovely garden, with beautiful flowers and fruit trees (apples of course, it’s Normandie, and pears). She just brought me a full set of food after knowing I hadn’t had my dinner. What a treat, she even offered a very nice home-made chocolate mousse. We sat together in the balcony, she introduced me her lovely garden, and showed me the pictures through 4 seasons. I really wished I could stay several days more! but the trip plan was await, and that is the last etape to Le Havre and I had to catch the train back and head towards Svalbard.

So I got some short break at Le Havre. finally, sitting at the beach, watching seagulls and hearing the wind (and having some very fresh seafood, bien sur)

Oh this log is already too long, I should write things about Svalbard in the next log.
